# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: October 7, 2026**

The **Diocese of Nike** (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the **Diocese of Nike** mobile application (the “App”). The App is designed to help members and users stay connected with the Diocese, manage church groups, view and participate in church activities, and receive information about upcoming events.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our App.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect the following information:

### Personal Information

When you create an account or use certain features, we may collect:

* Full name
* Email address
* Phone number
* Password or authentication information
* Profile photograph, if you choose to provide one
* Church group or ministry membership
* Church-related roles or responsibilities
* Other information you voluntarily provide through the App

### Church Group Information

If you join or participate in a church group, we may collect information relating to your participation, such as:

* Group or ministry membership
* Attendance or participation information
* Group activities
* Group communications
* Information you voluntarily submit to group administrators

### Event Information

When you register for or participate in an event, we may collect information necessary to manage the event, such as:

* Your name
* Contact information
* Event registration details
* Attendance information
* Other information you voluntarily provide for the event

### Device and Usage Information

We may automatically collect limited technical information when you use the App, including:

* Device type and operating system
* App version
* IP address
* General usage information
* Crash reports and diagnostic information

We use this information primarily to maintain, secure, and improve the App.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

* Create and manage your account
* Manage church groups and ministries
* Help you participate in church groups and activities
* Display upcoming church events
* Process event registrations
* Communicate important church announcements
* Send notifications relating to groups, events, or your account
* Provide support and respond to inquiries
* Maintain the security of the App
* Improve the functionality and user experience of the App
* Comply with applicable legal obligations

We will not use your personal information for purposes that are incompatible with those described in this Privacy Policy without providing appropriate notice.

## 3. Church Community Information

The App may allow certain information to be visible to other authorized members of the Diocese or members of the same church group.

For example, your name, profile information, group membership, or participation in church activities may be visible to other users where necessary for the operation of the relevant church group or feature.

We encourage you not to submit information that you do not want other authorized members or church administrators to see.

## 4. Notifications

The App may send push notifications about:

* Upcoming church events
* Group activities
* Church announcements
* Changes to events or group activities
* Important account or App information

You can control certain notification permissions through your device settings.

## 5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We may share information in limited circumstances, including:

### Within the Diocese

Information may be accessible to authorized Diocese administrators, church group leaders, ministry leaders, or other authorized personnel when necessary to manage church activities and services.

### Service Providers

We may use trusted third-party service providers to help operate the App, such as providers of:

* Cloud hosting
* Authentication
* Database services
* Push notifications
* Analytics
* Technical support

These providers may process personal information on our behalf and are expected to protect it appropriately.

### Legal Requirements

We may disclose information where reasonably necessary to:

* Comply with applicable laws or legal processes
* Respond to lawful requests from authorities
* Protect the rights, safety, or property of the Diocese, our users, or others
* Detect or prevent fraud, abuse, or security threats

## 6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no internet-based service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should use a strong password and avoid sharing your account credentials with others.

## 7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain church records, fulfill legitimate administrative purposes, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and enforce our agreements.

When information is no longer required, we may securely delete or anonymize it, subject to applicable legal and administrative requirements.

## 8. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:

* Request access to personal information we hold about you
* Request correction of inaccurate information
* Request deletion of certain personal information
* Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
* Object to certain uses of your information
* Request information about how your personal information is processed

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the contact information provided below.

Some information may need to be retained where we have a legitimate legal, security, or administrative reason to do so.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The App is intended to be used in connection with the activities of the Diocese and may be used by members of different age groups.

Where the App collects information from children or minors, we encourage parents or legal guardians to be involved in their use of the App where appropriate.

We do not knowingly collect more personal information from children than is reasonably necessary for the relevant church activity or service.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.

## 10. Third-Party Services and Links

The App may use third-party services or contain links to external websites or services.

Those third parties may have their own privacy policies and terms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services that we do not control.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of third-party services before providing them with personal information.

## 11. International Data Transfers

Some service providers used to operate the App may store or process information outside Nigeria.

Where personal information is transferred to another country, we will take reasonable steps to ensure that the information receives appropriate protection in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

## 12. Nigerian Data Protection

The Diocese of Nike is located in Nigeria and seeks to comply with applicable Nigerian data protection requirements, including the **Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023**, where applicable.

We aim to process personal information fairly, lawfully, and transparently and to take reasonable steps to protect personal information against unauthorized or unlawful processing.

## 13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, our practices, or applicable laws.

When we make material changes, we may provide notice through the App or other appropriate means.

The “Last Updated” date at the beginning of this Privacy Policy indicates when the policy was most recently updated.
